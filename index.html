    Places where you have to ask permission are left off this map. Data comes from OpenStreetMap, loaded live — blank fields mean nobody has checked yet, not that the answer is no. Street addresses for unnamed toilets are reverse-geocoded from their coordinates, so they name the street, not a doorway. Food-court counters inside a mall are left out — the mall's own toilet is shown instead — and so are takeaway kiosks, which have nowhere to put one.
